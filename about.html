Meet Mike

Mike grew up in Grand Junction washing dishes at his grandfather's pizzeria on weekends. Twenty years later, he traded the dish pit for a converted step van and a wood-fired oven — because the best pizza, he figures, should come to you.

Every pie starts with dough made fresh that morning, sauce simmered in small batches, and toppings sourced from Western Slope growers whenever the season allows. No freezers, no shortcuts, no pizza that's been sitting under a heat lamp.

how it all started

The Short Version

2016

Learns the ropes at his grandfather's restaurant, first as dish crew, later running the pizza station.

2019

Buys a used step van and spends a winter building out a wood-fired oven in the back with a buddy from trade school.

2021

First official weekend at the Downtown Grand Junction farmers market — sells out of dough by 1pm.

Today

Rolling around Grand Junction most weekends, with a soft spot for anything topped with Palisade peaches.

mike, in his own words

"I just want people to taste the difference."

"I'm not trying to reinvent pizza. I just think it tastes better when someone who cares is making it a few feet from you, with a fire going and real ingredients — not a heat lamp and a bag of pre-shredded cheese. If I can bring that to a parking lot near your house, that's a good day."

When he's not in the truck, Mike's usually scouting the next stop, testing a seasonal topping idea, or arguing with regulars about whether pineapple belongs on pizza (his answer: only on the build-your-own).
